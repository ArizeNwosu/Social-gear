CASE STUDIES

See it working
before we talk.

Before a first meeting, we build a working tool from public information about the industry. Each one below is a working build you can open and try, made from public records and listings, not client data.

COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE · INDUSTRIAL

Manufacturing demand radar

Aerospace and defense companies are running out of buildings in the South Bay. This board finds who is likely to need manufacturing space next in the San Fernando Valley, Santa Clarita and Ventura County. It shows the public signals behind each lead, keeps facts and guesses apart, checks listed buildings against what these tenants need, and drafts a first email for the broker to review.

COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE · LAND USE

Zoning Radar

Rezonings and new specific plans create deals before most brokers hear about them. Every week this brief scans Planning Commission, City Council and Board agendas plus state environmental notices across the Conejo Valley and Ventura County. It keeps only the items that change what land can be used for, explains why each one matters and points to who to call, with a link to the source record.

ANY BUSINESS · EMAIL

Inbox Pulse

A one-page map of where an inbox's time goes: how much arrives, who fills it, what share of real people actually get a reply, and which threads are waiting on you right now. The AI only reads; it never sends or deletes anything. We run it on our own companies' inboxes. This version uses a fictional business and sample data.
