What Happens in Your First Thirty Days with Social Gear AI

October 19, 2026

Starting something new with an outside partner can feel uncertain, especially when the subject is AI and you're not sure what the process involves. Here's a straightforward walk-through of what the first thirty days with Social Gear AI actually look like.

In week one, we have a kickoff conversation. This isn't a sales call — it's a working session. We learn about your business, your team structure, your current tools, and the tasks that take the most time. We ask a lot of questions and take notes. By the end of the conversation, we've usually identified two or three areas where AI could be useful. We also walk you through the time map exercise and ask your team to track their tasks for the following week.

In week two, we review the time map results together. This is where the picture sharpens. We look at the data, identify the highest-impact opportunity, and agree on a first project. It might be automating email drafts, building a proposal generator, or setting up a lead-routing workflow. We scope it tightly — one workflow, one team member, one clear outcome.

Weeks three and four are where the build happens. We configure the tools, connect them to your existing systems, and set up the workflow with human review built in. We run test cases with your team, refine the prompts and rules based on what we see, and document the process so it's repeatable. By the end of week four, the workflow is live and your team is using it in their daily work.

At the thirty-day mark, we do a check-in. How much time is the workflow saving? What's working well, and what needs adjustment? Is the team comfortable, or do they need more training? That conversation shapes what comes next — whether it's refining the first workflow, expanding to a second one, or simply continuing with ongoing support.

The first step is a conversation. If you'd like to see what your first thirty days could look like, let's talk.
