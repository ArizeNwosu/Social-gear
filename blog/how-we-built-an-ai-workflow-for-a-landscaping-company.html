How We Built an AI Workflow for a Landscaping Company

July 8, 2026

A landscaping company came to us with a familiar problem. Their owner was spending an hour or more writing each project proposal by hand — pulling together pricing, scope descriptions, timelines, and terms from memory and past documents. With a growing client list, the bottleneck was costing them new business.

We started with a time map. The owner and two crew leads tracked their tasks for a week. The results confirmed the bottleneck: proposal writing was eating into every evening, and follow-up emails were falling through the cracks. The crew leads, meanwhile, were spending time texting photos and job notes that never made it into a central system.

The first workflow we built was simple. We created a structured intake form — a short questionnaire the owner fills in after a site visit, covering property size, services requested, special conditions, and budget range. That form feeds into an AI system trained on the company's past proposals, pricing tiers, and preferred language. Within seconds, it generates a professional proposal draft that the owner reviews, adjusts, and sends.

The second piece addressed the crew communication gap. We set up a workflow where crew leads text a photo and a few notes to a dedicated number. The system logs the update, attaches it to the right job, and sends the owner a daily summary. No app to install, no new habit to learn — just texting, which they were already doing.

Within three weeks, proposal turnaround dropped from a day or two to under an hour. The owner reported getting more sleep. The follow-up emails started going out consistently, and close rates improved because clients were getting responses while the conversation was still fresh. None of this required exotic technology. It required understanding the business and building around what was already there.
