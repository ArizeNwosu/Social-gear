Five Workflows Every Small Business Can Automate This Month

September 23, 2026

You don't need a strategic roadmap or a technology overhaul to start automating. These five workflows are common to nearly every small business, well-suited to AI, and achievable within a few weeks.

First: meeting follow-ups. After every client meeting, someone on your team spends time writing a summary email with next steps. An AI workflow can listen to meeting notes or a brief voice memo, generate the summary, and draft the follow-up email. Review it, adjust, and send. What used to take fifteen minutes per meeting now takes three.

Second: lead intake and routing. When a new inquiry comes in — through your website form, email, or phone — an AI workflow can categorize it, pull out the key details, and route it to the right person with a pre-drafted response. The lead gets a faster reply, and your team doesn't spend time on triage.

Third: weekly reporting. If your team produces a recurring report — a sales summary, a project status update, a client activity log — the data collection and formatting can be automated. AI pulls the numbers from your existing tools, structures the report, and delivers a draft for review. The analysis and commentary stay human; the assembly doesn't need to be.

Fourth: appointment reminders and confirmations. Sending reminders, handling reschedule requests, and confirming bookings is pure repetition. An AI workflow can manage the entire sequence, escalating to a person only when something unusual happens — like a client asking a question that doesn't fit the standard flow.

Fifth: document drafting. Proposals, onboarding packets, scopes of work, and standard agreements all follow templates. An AI system trained on your past documents can generate first drafts from a few inputs, saving your team the time of assembling each one from scratch. The content stays yours; the assembly becomes instant.

Pick one of these five and start there. Or, if you'd like help choosing, we're happy to walk through your options.
