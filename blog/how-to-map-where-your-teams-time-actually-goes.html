How to Map Where Your Team's Time Actually Goes

May 27, 2026

Most business owners have an intuitive sense of where their team's time goes. But intuition and reality often diverge in surprising ways. A time map is a simple exercise that makes the invisible visible — and it's the foundation of every good AI strategy.

The process is straightforward. Ask each team member to log their tasks for one typical week, noting roughly how long each one takes and whether the work is creative, administrative, or communication. You're not tracking productivity. You're building a picture of how the business actually runs day to day. The goal is honesty, not performance measurement.

What usually surfaces is a set of tasks that take far more cumulative time than anyone realized. Data entry, scheduling, formatting documents, writing routine emails, updating spreadsheets — these tasks individually seem small, but when you add them up across a team, they can account for twenty or thirty percent of total working hours. That's where AI has the most immediate value.

A time map also reveals tasks that shouldn't be automated. Anything that involves nuanced judgment, relationship building, or creative problem-solving is better left to people. The map helps you draw the line clearly, so you're not trying to automate the wrong things.

We walk every client through this exercise before recommending any tools or workflows. It takes about a week, costs nothing, and gives you a clear, honest picture of where improvement is possible. The decisions that follow are always better for it.
