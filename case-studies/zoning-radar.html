South Oxnard Connect Specific Plan

Oxnard · Saviers Rd corridor, Hueneme Rd to Van Ness Ave/Dollie St
Hearing Nov 5
ResidentialMixed useRezoningHousing elementTransit corridor

The City is proposing a specific plan for about 119 acres along the Gold Coast Transit Route 1 corridor. It raises development capacity on 13 designated opportunity sites and sets objective design standards. A voluntary tier program trades extra density for affordable units. The package includes a general plan and housing element amendment, a zoning map amendment and a Negative Declaration. That night the Commission made its recommendation to City Council.

Why it mattersMost of the corridor is existing strip retail. Once the plan passes, those owners can build housing or mixed use with objective standards, and many won't know it. Pull the 13 sites from the staff report and call the owners before Council votes.

Maulhardt-Stiles: up to 950 homes in East Village

Oxnard · Cesar Chavez Dr, Gibraltar St, Camino Del Sol, N. Rose Ave
Heard Oct 15 · Council next
ResidentialCommercialGeneral plan amendmentZone changeTentative mapEIR

A master-planned neighborhood on about 107 acres in the Northeast Community Specific Plan area. It allows up to 950 homes, up to 40,000 sq ft of commercial and 12.9 acres of parks. A tentative map creates master lots for phased development. The Planning Commission considered a recommendation to certify Final EIR No. 2026-01. City Council certification and approval come next.

Why it mattersMaster lots mean builder takedowns in phases, so land brokers and homebuilders should get in early. The 40,000 sq ft of commercial will need retail tenants. Nearby land just gained a large comparable.

Every Neighborhood Commercial parcel rezoned

County of Ventura · all unincorporated C1 parcels
Commission recommended · Board next
CommercialRezoningZoning code rewrite

The County is rewriting its non-coastal zoning ordinance. It removes the Commercial Office (CO) and Neighborhood Commercial (C1) zones and rezones every C1 parcel to Commercial Planned Development (CPD). The Planning Commission heard it September 17. The Board of Supervisors acts next.

Why it mattersEvery owner of a C1 parcel in the unincorporated county gets a new zone, with different allowed uses and permit paths. It's an easy reason to call owners and tenants: "your zoning is changing, here's what it means."

Amgen Building 50: 377,256 sq ft life-sciences building

Thousand Oaks · 701 Pauling Dr (former Building 14)
Approved · state notice Nov 2
R&D / life sciencesSpecific plan amendmentDevelopment permit

A new four-story technology and life-sciences building with a basement, loading dock and site work. It is paired with amendments to the Amgen Center Specific Plan (SP-16), including its height district map. The Planning Commission heard it September 14. The City filed its state Notice of Determination on November 2.

Why it mattersThis is the largest commercial project in the Conejo this cycle. Expect demand for nearby housing, contractors and suppliers, and service retail. It is also a signal for life-science space across the 101 corridor.

Moorpark Downtown Specific Plan adopted

Moorpark · downtown core
Approved · state notice Oct 23
ResidentialCommercialRezoningGeneral plan amendment

Moorpark filed its Notice of Determination for the Downtown Specific Plan. The plan implements General Plan 2050 and includes a zoning code amendment, a general plan amendment and a zoning map amendment.

Why it mattersDowntown parcels now sit under new rules. The state notice doesn't say which parcels gained capacity, so read the plan's land-use map before calling. That one gap is the reason this brief needs the city's own documents and not just state filings.
