The real problem we are solving

Most digital products fail for one reason: the creator builds for months, opens the cart, and nobody is there. The product is not the problem. The missing audience is.

A landing page alone does not fix that. Three things must work together:

  1. A reason to sign up today. "Coming soon" does not earn an email address. A clear promise and a founding-member benefit do.
  2. A reason to stay. A welcome email and your newsletters turn a stranger into someone who trusts you before you ask for money.
  3. A steady flow of the right visitors. The page converts traffic. It does not create it. Your social content and your network bring the people.

This blueprint covers all three. It also gives you something most creators never get: proof of demand before you finish the product. If vocal coaches sign up and reply to your emails, you know what to build. If they do not, you find out in weeks, not after months of filming.

Your logo, applied. You provide the logo. We build the page colors, type and layout around it, so the brand looks consistent from the first visit.

More Money More Music is its own brand, separate from angelamichael.com. Your personal site stays your studio and artist home. This brand is the business school for coaches. Each one links to the other.

The funnel page, section by section

One page, one goal: the visitor gives an email address. Every section either moves them toward that or is removed. There is no menu and no links that lead away.

  1. Headline and promise. One clear line on the result: how vocal coaches earn more without teaching more hours. A sub-line names who it is for.
  2. Your video. You, on camera, 2 to 3 minutes. It plays above the fold on phone and desktop.
  3. Email form, right beside the video. First name and email only. Each extra field lowers sign-ups.
  4. The founding list. What they get for signing up now: first access when the doors open, founding-member pricing, and your newsletter. When your free guide is ready, we add it here as an instant download.
  5. The problem, in their words. Full calendar, flat income, no time for their own music. They must feel understood before they act.
  6. Meet Angela. Your story, credits and photo. Short. Proof, not a biography.
  7. What is coming. A preview of the program modules.
  8. Proof. Student and coach results as you collect them. We build the section now and fill it over time.
  9. Your social feed. Recent posts from your Instagram, placed low on the page so they add trust without pulling visitors away before they sign up.
  10. Second form and final call. The same offer, repeated for visitors who scrolled the full page.

Built to convert

  • Phone first. Most of your traffic will arrive from social apps.
  • Fast. Slow pages lose visitors before the video starts.
  • A thank-you page after sign-up that confirms their place on the list and asks one question: "What is your biggest struggle with earning from coaching?" The answers become your course outline and your sales copy.
  • Tracking installed from day one: visits, video plays, sign-ups, and where each visitor came from.

The video

Phase 1 uses a short video, 2 to 3 minutes, because the ask is small: an email address. The long sales video comes in Phase 2, when the ask is a purchase.

Script structure

Part Time What you say
Hook 0:00 to 0:15 The problem in one line. "If your calendar is full and your income is flat, this is for you."
Credibility 0:15 to 0:45 Who you are and why you can teach this. Two or three facts, no more.
The shift 0:45 to 1:30 The one idea that changes how a coach earns. Give real value here.
The offer 1:30 to 2:15 What you are building, and why to join the list now.
Call to action 2:15 to 2:30 "Enter your email below to get first access."

What you do: record the video and send us the footage. Use a recent phone, a quiet room and window light. Real and clear beats polished and stiff. The structure above is your guide.

What we do: edit your footage, tighten the pacing, add captions and your logo, and set it up for fast playback on the page.

The email engine

The list is the asset. Social platforms can change their rules or cut your reach. Your email list is yours.

Capture. The page form sends each new subscriber to Klaviyo, tagged with the source they came from.

Your Klaviyo account. You open a Klaviyo account for More Money More Music, and we configure it. It is your account and your list.

Welcome email, automatic. One email, sent the moment someone signs up. It thanks them, tells them what is coming, and asks them to reply with their biggest struggle in earning from coaching. Each reply is market research in the customer's own words.

Newsletter. You write and send a newsletter when you have something to say. There is no fixed schedule at this stage.

Launch sequence (Phase 2). When the product is ready, a planned run of emails opens the doors to this list first.

Deliverability. We authenticate your sending domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so your emails arrive in the inbox, not in spam.

Klaviyo is free for the first 250 contacts and 500 emails per month. As the list grows, it moves to a paid plan that starts at about $20 per month (Klaviyo pricing).

Traffic: how people find the page

The page turns visitors into subscribers. These five sources bring the visitors. None of them needs an ad budget.

  1. Link in bio. Every Instagram and TikTok profile points to moremoneymoremusic.com.
  2. Short videos with one call to action. Tips for coaches, each ending with "join the founding list, link in bio."
  3. Your existing network. Students, fellow singers, session colleagues and teachers you know. One personal email or message from you is the fastest first 100 subscribers.
  4. Where coaches already gather. Teacher associations, Facebook groups, forums and podcasts for voice teachers. Invite them to join the founding list.
  5. angelamichael.com. A banner and a link from your current site.

A simple rhythm works: three short posts per week and one personal outreach push in week one. Paid ads are an option later, once the page has proven its sign-up rate with free traffic.
