Good questions

AI can feel like a lot. Let’s make it simple.

A few things people often ask before we get started.

What does a fractional Chief AI Officer do?

We help you make smart decisions about AI without hiring a full-time executive. That means setting priorities, choosing tools, guiding implementation, and helping your team use them well.

Do we need to be technical?

Not at all. We translate the technology into practical steps and work alongside your team at the level that makes sense for you.

Will AI replace our people?

Our goal is to give people more time for the work that needs their judgment, creativity, and relationships. We design tools to support your team and keep humans in control.

Which AI tools do you work with?

We’re tool agnostic. We’ll recommend solutions based on your goals, existing systems, budget, and the way your team works.

How do you handle privacy and security?

We start by understanding your requirements, data, and risk tolerance. We’ll help you evaluate vendors and configure tools with sensible safeguards for your business.

What if we don’t know where to start?

That’s exactly what the free clarity session is for. Bring your questions, a workflow that’s driving you nuts, or just your curiosity.
